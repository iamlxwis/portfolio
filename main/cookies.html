cookies.

what gets saved and why.

local storage

we use browser storage for your cookie choice, brat settings and designs, theme preferences, and saved favorites. local storage isn't a cookie, but it remembers things between visits.

your options

essential only and accept both save your choice. neither turns on extra tracking right now.

other sites

fonts, images, and other content may load from third parties. our cookie buttons don't control their requests or storage.

change it

use cookie settings at the bottom of the page, or clear the site's data in your browser.
