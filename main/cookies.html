cookies.

not the chocolate chip kind. unfortunately.

what we use

Our site uses browser local storage to keep your cookie preference, BRAT designs and settings, theme choices, and saved gallery favorites. Local storage isn't technically a cookie, but it can remember things between visits.

essential vs optional

essential only saves your privacy choice and still lets you use the site's built-in features. accept also saves your preference; it does not currently enable any extra tracking because we have no optional tracking tools wired to these buttons.

third-party stuff

Embedded or externally loaded resources (such as Google Fonts, GitHub content, and image hosts) may have their own policies. Our choice buttons cannot control those third-party requests.

change your mind

Tap cookie settings at the bottom of any page to change your choice. You can also clear this site's storage in your browser settings.
