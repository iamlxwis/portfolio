tips & how to use

1. start with your text. type anything and the editor will fit it into the canvas.

2. doesn't look right? try adjusting line spacing under more. play with it until the text wraps the way you want.

3. make it yours. pick a preset or customize the background and text colors.

4. save it. tap export when you're happy with it.
