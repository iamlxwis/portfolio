privacy.

your business stays your business. here's what this site actually does.

what gets stored

Some pages use your browser's local storage for things like saved favorites, BRAT editor settings, theme preferences, and your privacy choice. Those settings stay in your browser unless you share or export them.

what gets sent

When you visit the site, the hosting provider may process standard technical request data such as your IP address, browser details, and timestamps to deliver the pages and protect the service. Some pages request content from third parties, including image services, Google Fonts, and GitHub, which may receive your IP address and request details.

your uploads

Images selected in the BRAT editor are processed in your browser for editing. This site does not intentionally upload your chosen images to its own server. You control anything you choose to download or share.

your choices

You can clear saved site data in your browser settings at any time. The cookie notice remembers your choice in local storage. No optional advertising or analytics cookies are enabled by this site's own consent widget at present.

questions?

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through the contact details linked on the homepage or the GitHub profile.
