privacy.

here's what happens with your data.

saved on your device

some pages use local storage for preferences, saved favorites, brat designs, and your cookie choice. it stays in your browser unless you export or share it.

site visits

our hosting provider may process your ip address, browser details, and request times to run and protect the site.

outside services

some pages load fonts, images, or content from services like google fonts, github, and image hosts. those services may receive request details and have their own privacy policies.

brat uploads

images you pick for brat are edited in your browser. we don't intentionally upload them to our own server.

your choice

you can clear saved site data in your browser settings. the cookie popup saves your preference. it doesn't currently turn on any optional ad or analytics tracking.

questions

reach out through the homepage or github.
