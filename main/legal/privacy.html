Privacy.

Data, storage, and third-party services.

Updated 20 November 2026

Contents

01Browser storage

Favorites, BRAT settings and designs, and your privacy choice can be saved in your browser. You can clear site data in your browser settings.

02Hosting data

The hosting provider may process your IP address, browser information, and visit times to deliver and protect the website.

03External services

Some pages load fonts, images, or content from Google Fonts, GitHub, and image hosts. Those services may receive request details under their own policies.

04Image uploads

BRAT edits uploaded images in your browser. We do not intentionally send those uploads to our server. Exported or shared content is controlled by you.

05Tracking

The privacy controls currently do not enable optional advertising or analytics trackers. Both options only save your preference.

06Contact

Questions can be directed through GitHub.
